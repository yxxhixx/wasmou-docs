**Wasmou for WooCommerce** turns your WordPress shop into a Wasmou reseller store. Your customers pay you, the plugin buys from your Wasmou wallet and delivers the code, subscription or top-up to your customer. No coding.

## Requirements

- WordPress 6.0+ and WooCommerce 7.0+ (PHP 7.4 or newer)
- A Wasmou account with an API key ([get it here](https://myapp.wasmou.net/app/api-access)) and funds in your wallet
- Compatible with High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS) and the block-based Cart and Checkout

## Install in 3 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload and activate">
    WordPress admin > **Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin**, choose the zip, then **Activate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Wasmou account">
    Open **WooCommerce > Wasmou**, paste your API key and press **Test connection**. You should see your wallet balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import and sell">
    Open the **Import products** tab, pick what you want to sell and press **Import selected**. Products arrive with images, descriptions, warranty and activation guides.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pricing

Wasmou prices are in Algerian dinar. In the **Pricing** tab choose your margin (a percentage or a fixed amount), your store currency and exchange rate, and a rounding rule. Prices are recalculated at every sync and never drop below your cost.

## How orders are delivered

1. A customer pays you in WooCommerce.
2. The plugin purchases each Wasmou item from your wallet. Every purchase carries an `Idempotency-Key`, so a retry never buys twice.
3. As soon as Wasmou has the code, it appears on the order page, in My Account and in the order e-mail, with a **Copy** button and the activation guide.
4. The order is marked **Completed** (you can turn this off).

Products that are added to the customer's own account (subscriptions) ask for the details they need at checkout, for example the e-mail of the account. They are activated shortly after, and the page tells the customer so.

## Safety built in

- **Price protection**: if the Wasmou price rose above what the customer would pay, the cart asks them to review it instead of selling at a loss.
- **Wallet check** at checkout, with an e-mail alert when your balance is low.
- **No double purchases**: concurrent requests, crashes and network errors can not buy the same item twice.
- **If Wasmou can not deliver**, the order is put **On hold** and you are e-mailed, or the failed items are refunded automatically through your payment gateway.
- Stock is mirrored from Wasmou, so you never sell what is out of stock.

## Keep prices and stock fresh

The plugin syncs every 15 minutes by default (**Delivery** tab). Re-syncing only touches prices, stock and plans: your edits to titles, descriptions and categories are kept. On low-traffic sites, add a real cron job that calls `wp-cron.php` every minute for the fastest delivery.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "API key rejected" | Check the key. If you restricted it to IP addresses, allow your hosting server IP or clear the list. |
| Orders stay **On hold** | Open the order: the **Wasmou** box shows the reason and a **Retry failed items** button. Most often the wallet needs funds. |
| Products show out of stock | They are out of stock at Wasmou. Run **Sync prices and stock now** in the **Status** tab. |
| Delivery is slow | Make sure WP-Cron runs (see **Status** tab). |

## For developers

The plugin uses the public API only: [catalog](/services/catalog), [account balance](/services/account), [purchase](/services/products) and [order retrieval](/services/orders). It sends an `Idempotency-Key` header on every purchase (see [Automate fulfilment](/guides/automate-fulfilment)).
