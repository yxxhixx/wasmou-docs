---
title: "Pricing and balance"
description: "What you pay and how your wallet works."
---

- All amounts are in **DZD**.
- The `price` in the catalog is **your** price, with your reseller terms applied. Other accounts may see different prices for the same variant.
- Your wallet has a `balance` and an `available_balance`. The available balance is what you can spend right now (balance minus amounts reserved by pending operations). Use `available_balance` for decisions.
- Prices of automatic products (gift cards, top-ups) follow our supplier: they are computed from the supplier's USD price and can change between catalog refreshes. Refresh the catalog every 5 to 15 minutes.
- A purchase debits the wallet when it is accepted. Failed or canceled orders are refunded automatically.

Add funds from the [app](https://myapp.wasmou.net/deposit): Baridi Mob / CCP transfer.

<Warning>
A purchase fails with `Insufficient balance` if your available balance is lower than `price × quantity`. Check `GET /balance` before a large batch.
</Warning>
