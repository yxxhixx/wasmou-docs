---
title: "Delivery modes"
description: "What happens after you buy, depending on the product."
---

Every variant in the catalog has a `delivery` field.

| `delivery` | What it means | When you get the code |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `instant` | Gift cards and vouchers fulfilled automatically by our supplier. | Within seconds. Poll the order until `status` is `completed`. |
| `topup` | Game credit or Telegram Premium / Stars sent straight to the account you name. | Within seconds to a few minutes. No code: the order becomes `completed` when delivered. |
| `key` | Wasmou holds a pool of codes. One is assigned the moment you buy. | In the purchase flow. `in_stock` tells you how many are left. |
| `manual` | A person delivers the product (account top-up, activation, subscription). | When the team marks it delivered. The order stays `processing` until then. |

<Note>
The catalog now holds hundreds of gift cards and game top-ups delivered automatically. A few products stay `manual`. Check `delivery` after each catalog refresh instead of hard-coding it: a product can switch between `instant` and `manual` when our supplier is out of stock.
</Note>

## Order statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `processing` | Accepted, waiting for fulfilment. |
| `completed` | Delivered (instant and key products). When the product has a code, `has_key` is `true` and `key` holds it. |
| `delivered` | Delivered by the team (manual products). `key` holds the code or details when there is one. |
| `failed` | Could not be fulfilled. The amount is refunded to your wallet. |
| `refunded` | Refunded after processing. |
| `canceled` | Canceled by the team (manual products). Refunded. |

A status other than `processing` is final.

Poll `POST /products/orders/retrieve` every 10 to 30 seconds for `processing` orders, and stop when the status is final. See [Automate fulfilment](/guides/automate-fulfilment).
